## 0.1.0

- **Wheel Choice 1.3.0 Compatibility**:
  - Upgraded dependency to `wheel_choice: ^1.3.0`.
  - Re-exported all primary APIs from `wheel_choice` directly from `package:wheel_choice_ext/wheel_choice_ext.dart`.
- **Date Picker Suite (`src/date/`)**:
  - `WheelDateChoice`, `WheelDateController`, `WheelDateChoiceFormField`.
  - Added `value` getter, `valueListenable`, step helpers, and automatic day clamping.
  - Subtree theming inheritance with `WheelChoiceTheme`.
- **Time Picker Suite (`src/time/`)**:
  - `WheelTimeChoice`, `WheelTimeController`, `WheelTimeChoiceFormField`.
  - Supported 24-hour mode (0..23) and 12-hour mode (1..12 + AM/PM).
  - Configurable `minuteStep` (1, 5, 10, 15 mins) and step navigation.
- **Combined Date & Time Suite (`src/datetime/`)**:
  - `WheelDateTimeChoice`, `WheelDateTimeController`, `WheelDateTimeChoiceFormField`.
  - Synchronized date and time wheels into a unified `DateTime` model.
- **Duration & Timer Suite (`src/duration/`)**:
  - `WheelDurationChoice`, `WheelDurationController`, `WheelDurationChoiceFormField`.
  - Hours, minutes, and seconds wheels for stopwatches, workouts, and cooking timers.
- **Number & Weight/Height Suite (`src/number/`)**:
  - `WheelNumberChoice`, `WheelNumberController`, `WheelNumberChoiceFormField`.
  - Multi-column integer, decimal fraction, and selectable unit wheels.
- **Date Range Booking Suite (`src/range/`)**:
  - `WheelDateRangeChoice`, `WheelDateRangeController`, `WheelDateRangeChoiceFormField`.
  - Dual calendar date wheels with automatic boundary synchronization (`startDate <= endDate`).
- **Cascading Selection Suite (`src/cascade/`)**:
  - `WheelCascadeChoice<T>`, `WheelCascadeController<T>`, `WheelCascadeChoiceFormField<T>`.
  - Multi-level dependent tree options (e.g. Country → State → City).
- **Modal Dialog & Bottom Sheet Helpers (`src/dialog/`)**:
  - `showWheelDatePicker`, `showWheelTimePicker`, `showWheelDateTimePicker`, `showWheelDurationPicker`.
  - `showWheelModalSheet`: generic bottom sheet container with Cancel, Done, and custom wheel content.
- **Showcase Example**:
  - Overhauled `example/lib/main.dart` with 6 dedicated interactive tabs.
- **Tests**:
  - Comprehensive unit and widget test suite covering all 7 widget categories (47 tests passing).
